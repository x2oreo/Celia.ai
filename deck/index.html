A heart-safety agent for people with Long QT syndrome. It checks every medicine, watches your heart rate on the wrist, and speaks for you in an emergency.

Native HarmonyOS, phone + watch · by Double Oreo

The problem

1 in 2,000 people have Long QT syndrome.

Their heart takes longer to recharge between beats. An ordinary antibiotic, an anti-sickness pill or a sudden alarm can tip it into a rhythm that stops it. Most of the time they feel nothing and look healthy.

Each dot is one person · ● one has LQTS

The hidden trigger

250+

medicines can lengthen the QT. Harmless for most people; for them, possibly the last prescription.

Three types

Effort · Startle · Sleep

LQT1 swimming, LQT2 an alarm clock, LQT3 a slow heart at night.

Torsades de pointes

150–300

chaotic beats a minute. It can stop on its own, or stop the heart.

Time to act

4–6 min

without CPR. Most GPs never meet a patient with LQTS.
